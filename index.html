About Our Well Project

Our most pressing goal is to dig a well on the Agape land and install a submersible pump. This critical infrastructure would help mitigate against drought conditions, enable vegetable growing during the dry season, and support year-round agricultural training.

The estimated cost for the well and pump is $10,000, with good water available about 65 feet down.
